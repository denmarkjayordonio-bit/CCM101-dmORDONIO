## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Amazon Web Services (AWS). I liked it because it has many services that can be used for different types of projects. I also liked that resources can be increased or decreased depending on the needs of the application or business. For me, AWS is flexible and useful especially if a company is growing.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP are all similar cloud platforms that provide services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and security. They also have options for scalability and have different tools that can help businesses and developers. Although the names of their services and the way they are used are different, their main purpose is the same: to provide cloud resources online.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements have a big impact on the choice of cloud platform. You first need to know what your company needs, such as budget, security, performance, existing technology, storage, and scalability. For example, if a company is already using a lot of Microsoft products, Azure may be more practical. If the focus is on AI and machine learning, GCP may be a better fit.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

I learned that it is not enough to just know about different cloud services. You also need to understand the needs and problems of the client before recommending a platform. I also learned how to compare AWS, Azure, and GCP and choose the most suitable solution depending on the situation of a business.
